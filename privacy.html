Privacy

Your privacy matters.

A straightforward explanation of how this website handles information.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

This website introduces Singleton-Campbell Limited, a UK software development company.

Information you enter

The contact and StageCom testing forms send the information you enter to FormSubmit, which processes the submission and forwards it to office@singleton-campbell.co.uk. This includes your name, email address and message, plus any role, venue or setup details you provide. We use this information to respond to your enquiry or discuss testing arrangements. Please avoid including sensitive information. The forms do not save your details in browser storage.

Cookies and analytics

Our website does not set cookies, use advertising trackers or run analytics scripts. Form submissions are handled on FormSubmit’s service, where cookies and a security check such as reCAPTCHA may be used to prevent abuse. Your browser, hosting provider and form service may process technical information needed to deliver and protect these services.

Form delivery

FormSubmit handles form delivery as an external service. Submitting a form sends its contents to that provider; a security check and confirmation may be displayed on its website. Read FormSubmit’s privacy information. You can alternatively email office@singleton-campbell.co.uk directly.

Hosting and external services

The website is prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request details, to operate and secure its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

The site uses fonts available on your device and does not request fonts from an external font service.

Your rights

UK data protection law provides rights over personal information, including access, correction, erasure and objection where applicable. You may also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Privacy enquiries

For privacy enquiries or to exercise your data protection rights, email office@singleton-campbell.co.uk.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice if the way this website handles information changes.
